STORY: Read the person and responsibilities, choose an actual project, inspect its media and implementation, then inspect career and contact destinations.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Ricky Choi name and factual introduction at left; approved Vuddy home and chat images at right, followed by direct project links. Mobile preserves introduction and image context.

FINISH: Current desktop and mobile inspection, independent review including the user's rejection, persistent copy method and regression checks, and updated token documentation.
