## Analysis translated into the design

The existing site already contains useful ownership and technical detail. Its first viewport spends most of its space on a broad engineer introduction and a decorative illustration, so a reader must scroll before seeing representative products. The redesign uses real product screens as its visual identity and provides a direct path into the selected contribution case.

The selected working resume places more weight on runtime stability, architecture, AI development and verification. The public page keeps Android as the main hiring context while bringing AI work into the opening project selector and the working-method section. Claims are narrowed to documented ownership and observable handling decisions rather than unsupported quality or satisfaction improvements.

The reel is treated as a motion reference: button feedback communicates availability, a short screen reveal explains project replacement, and native expandable details let a reader control depth. These are interpretations of the observed examples, not a verified transcription of all three named skills. Motion does not gate access to text, and reduced-motion preferences disable the exhibition animation.
